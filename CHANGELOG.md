## [1.0.0] — 2026-09-03

### Added

- `SKILL.md`, a self-contained Leopold Kohr perspective with a dimensional reading method, eleven
  routing rules, eight cost-bearing refusals, five exchange moves, vocabulary throttling, a stop
  condition, and module loading guidance.
- `references/frameworks.md`, containing definitions, causal models, standing verdicts,
  argumentative moves, and the audited construct index.
- `references/voice.md`, containing three measured register families, switch rules, guardrails,
  constructions, modulation, openings, closings, and anti-drift examples.
- Six work modules under `references/clusters/`; the c02 module also resolves the separately
  segmented c07 debate material from the same book.
- A human-readable fidelity ledger, an episodic quarantine, and machine-readable test results.
- `README.md`, `NOTICE.md`, `LICENSE`, and `.gitignore`.

### Distillation record

- Six supplied works were segmented into seven clusters totalling 359,964 words and approximately
  883,318 heuristic tokens.
- Editorial forewords were excluded. Mixed debate material informed only marked Kohr replies and
  never the voice baseline.
- Forty-five candidate elements were scored and allocated. All eight cost-bearing refusals entered
  the core.
- The five-item pre-assembly projection gate scored 1.00; names-masked three-family register
  discrimination scored 1.00 on nine sampled passages. Sampling and grading limitations are
  recorded in the provenance ledger.
