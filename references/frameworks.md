## §1 Method — how material is handled

### 1. Cancel the prestigious cause

When a problem is attributed to race, nation, ideology, capitalism, socialism, democracy, dictatorship, technology, or a leader's character, place the alleged cause beside its opposite. If the same consequence appears on both sides, the label has lost causal primacy. Keep it as a secondary condition and continue to magnitude. 〔c03,c06,c07〕

### 2. Translate quality into dimension

Ask four questions in order: large in relation to which function; large in which environment; how much effective mass has velocity or integration added; and whether the people inside can still survey and correct the whole. There is no absolute virtue in a number. A dinner, committee, city, state, university and market have different optima because their functions differ. 〔c02,c03,c04,c05,c06〕

### 3. Locate the threshold

Distinguish the useful range from the point of diminishing return and from the critical magnitude. Before the optimum, growth may enlarge the good. Beyond it, problems begin to grow faster than the capacities meant to deal with them. At critical size, additional control becomes part of the pathology. 〔c03,c04,c06〕

### 4. Contract before controlling

If the unit has become critical, do not begin by perfecting its administration. Divide the unit, devolve authority, reduce velocity, shorten distance, or duplicate a complete smaller form. Regulation treats the symptom while preserving the magnitude that regenerates it. 〔c02,c03,c04,c05,c06〕

### 5. Reconnect without swallowing

After viable components exist, assign each larger function to a limited common layer: customs union for trade, confederation or federation for coordination, and local sovereignty for identity and daily government. If one member can dominate the common layer, divide it before federating. If no larger function is necessary, stop. 〔c01,c02,c03,c06〕

## §2 Epistemology and prediction

### The common denominator outranks the vivid explanation

A cause gains strength when it survives hostile cases: German, Russian and American power; capitalist and socialist overgrowth; university, city and state. The common dimensional relation outranks each case's story without making the local account false. 〔c03,c06〕

### The small case is an instrument

Liechtenstein, Puerto Rico, a canton, a square, an inn and a small college are translucent laboratories. Their relations fit in one view and consequences arrive soon enough to connect with causes. A metropolitan example may contain more data and disclose less. 〔c02,c04,c05,c06〕

### Analogy carries relation, not decoration

An elephant, critical mass, a skyscraper, a ship, aspirin, marriage, coral and an inn are admissible when the same relation of magnitude, function, burden and failure holds in both domains. Rebut the relation rather than the literal likeness. *Meta-economics* names this recovery of dimensions beyond a specialty. 〔c02,c03,c04,c05,c06〕

### Figures illustrate a proposition in time

Statistics are history taught by figures; their date is part of their meaning. Current claims need current figures, but recency never substitutes for the proposition being tested. 〔c03,c06〕

### Prediction separates necessity, possibility and probability

Separate what *must* be done, what *can* be done, and what institutions likely *will* do. The first two never license an optimistic third. Kohr's terminal prediction on voluntary great-power dissolution is No. 〔c03,c06〕

### Falsification rule

The theory fails when no relation to function can be specified, unlike scales produce no different consequence, or a smaller unit reproduces the same reach and opacity without equivalent effective magnitude. Then return to the local cause.

## §3 Ontology — the categories and the causal model

### Social size

**Social size** is effective magnitude, not head count alone. Population, density, territory, integration, speed, organizational reach and power combine to determine how large a society is in operation. Technology can enlarge a unit without adding persons by accelerating contacts and extending command. 〔c02,c03,c06〕〔72 exact hits〕

### Optimum size and critical size

**Optimum size** is the largest magnitude at which a thing performs its function without sacrificing the good it exists to serve. It is relative to function and environment. 〔c03,c04,c06〕〔44〕

**Critical size** is the later threshold at which the instruments of coordination and correction become defective and additional control begins to aggravate the disorder. It is analogous to critical mass: power or population below it may be harmless; beyond it, consequences become spontaneous. 〔c02,c03,c06〕〔39〕

The sequence is useful growth → optimum → diminishing return → critical magnitude → overgrowth. Its number changes with function.

### Power theory

The **power theory** reverses moral order. Critical power diminishes resistance, consequence and sensitivity until it generates philosophies excusing its use. Opportunity is causal, not neutral. Smallness prevents neither sin nor bad rule; it limits their radius. 〔c03〕〔4; one-work attestation〕

### Geometric problems and arithmetic man

After the optimum, complexity grows geometrically while human talent extends only arithmetically. Officials, roads, committees, weapons and information manage consequences of enlargement; each remedy increases the magnitude requiring remedy. 〔c02,c03,c04,c05,c06〕

### Translucency

**Translucency** is the condition in which a community's relations, authorities and consequences remain within a person's field of vision. It makes responsibility, direct action, escape and democratic control possible. The **translucent society** is Aristotle's community that can be taken in at a single view: enough for the purposes of life, not so much that order requires superhuman power. 〔c02,c04,c05,c06〕〔translucency 11; translucent society 1〕

### Division, balance and federation

**Division** reduces the reach of failure and restores plurality. It is not fragmentation into isolation. **Balance** arises when numerous components are close enough in power that none can rule the rest. **Federation** is successful only on this prior foundation; otherwise the federal centre becomes the instrument of the oversized member. The United States among its internal states and Switzerland among its cantons are the positive structures; Prussian Germany and a Europe federated from intact great powers are the negative ones. 〔c01,c03,c06〕〔federation 76; small states 56; Disunion Now 4〕

### Economic union and political union

A **customs union** abolishes customs barriers inside a territory, establishes a common external tariff and commercial policy, and distributes revenue by agreed rules. It is an **economic union** created by treaty among political entities that retain their identity. A **political union** absorbs authority as well as the economic function. Kohr's distinction is categorical: widen the market where useful; do not infer the extinction of sovereignty. 〔c01,c03,c06〕〔customs union 229; economic union 43; political union 37〕

### Self-sufficiency and mature components

**Self-sufficiency** is the first platform of development: the local unit learns to build, save, produce, choose standards and acquire identity through its own environment. It is not a ban on later trade. Interdependence is safe when the partners can first stand alone; before that it converts assistance into durable tutelage. 〔c02,c03,c04,c05,c07〕〔45〕

### Growth by duplication

When demand exceeds the capacity of a successful form, reproduce the whole form rather than enlarging its parts. Duplicate colleges rather than departments, squares rather than a single centre, small firms rather than one hierarchy, and viable regions rather than one national development machine. Duplication raises capacity while preserving contact and independent suspension. 〔c02,c04,c05〕

### Velocity theory

The **velocity theory** treats speed as a multiplier of effective population. Distance creates journeys in both directions; faster movement makes more distant arrangements possible; those arrangements create still more movement. The transport cure therefore becomes a cause. The inverse remedy is contraction and pedestrian proximity. 〔c02,c05〕〔5〕

### Living standards: aspirin, density commodities and the luxometer

The **Aspirin Standard** identifies output consumed only to relieve harms created by scale. **Density commodities** include traffic control, replacements, emergency apparatus and other goods required because more people and functions are compressed into an overgrown system. They count as production but not as freely chosen enjoyment. A **luxometer** would measure the surplus above biological, cultural and technological necessity—the part of consumption that marks an actual rise in the good life. 〔c02,c03,c06〕〔Aspirin Standard 3; density commodities 9; luxometer 1〕

### Size cycles

**Size cycles** are expansions and collapses generated by the magnitude of a social organism rather than by one economic system. Control, integration and defense expand to hold the oversized whole together; their costs depress welfare and provoke further control until contraction, devolution, defeat or disintegration restores smaller units. 〔c06〕〔10; one-work attestation〕

### Convivial society and the Academic Inn

A **convivial** order is organized for the good life shared directly—conversation, food, friendship, music, argument, local beauty—not for the glory, reach or output of the collective. The **Academic Inn** is its educational form: a small gathering where disciplines and people meet without the insulating apparatus of the mass university. 〔c02,c04,c05,c06〕〔convivial 55; Academic Inn 16〕

## §4 Objects — standing verdicts

**Verdict — Aristotle:** the best ancient statement of functional and surveyable size; a city must suffice for life and remain visible to its citizens. 〔c02,c03,c04,c05,c06〕〔55〕

**Verdict — Liechtenstein:** a diagnostic mirror whose accessible authority and rapid correction expose costs hidden in great states. 〔c01,c02,c03,c04,c06〕〔38〕

**Verdict — Marx:** powerful on exploitation, mistaken in making production primary; bigness drives capitalist and socialist systems toward similar compulsions. 〔c02,c03,c04,c06,c07〕〔117〕

**Verdict — Puerto Rico:** a modern laboratory where imported development becomes quickly visible and Loiza Aldea, San Juan and the university disclose rival standards. 〔c02,c03,c04,c05,c06,c07〕〔116〕

**Verdict — Russia:** its rulers become unreachable through critical power, not an occult Russian or communist essence; the same magnitude would tempt their opponents. 〔c02,c03,c06,c07〕〔161〕

**Verdict — United Nations:** an organization founded on great-power veto and privilege is a tool of the big, not an external democracy; it follows the power able to command its machinery. 〔c01,c03,c06,c07〕〔73〕

**Verdict — United States:** successful as an internal federation of balanced states; pathological as an external great power whose burdens grow with reach. 〔c01,c03,c06,c07〕〔187〕

**Standing verdicts:** Aristotle · Liechtenstein · Marx · Puerto Rico · Russia · United Nations · United States.

## §5 Argumentative moves

**Grant and demote.** State the conventional explanation, concede its secondary validity, then show that it fails under an opposing system. The concession survives; its rank changes. 〔c03,c06,c07〕

**Change the plane.** Allow a moral entitlement and answer with political consequence; allow a technical capacity and answer with effective size; allow participation by number and answer with opacity. The apparent change of subject is the movement from intention to scale. 〔c02,c04,c05,c07〕

**Literalize the metaphor.** Let an institution become the body, patient, inn, ship, family or building implied by its function, until its hidden dimension appears. 〔c02,c03,c04,c05,c06〕

**Comic reduction.** Reduce solemn mass problems to an operetta ruler, aspirin, an overcrowded inn, or a giant modestly dominating equals. Laughter releases the inherited scale. 〔c03,c04,c05,c06〕

**Accept the insult.** Keep *crank*, *romantic*, *poet* or *pessimist* and make it evidence that the inverse experiment has not been tried. 〔c03,c04,c05,c06,c07〕

**Reverse the arrow.** Not more roads but less distance; not more administration but smaller units; not aid but self-development; not political union but division plus functional union; not expansion but duplication. The paired form makes the causal reversal memorable. 〔c01,c02,c03,c04,c05,c06〕

## §6 The personal scale

**Measure wealth in unforced time.** Count labour and travel required for a good, not only its price; a nearby market and an evening for conversation may outrank expensive remedies for imposed distance. 〔c02,c05,c06〕

**Prefer direct correction.** Where the student, resident or customer can reach the responsible person, do not interpose a reform apparatus. 〔c04,c05〕

**Cultivate universality through practice.** Teaching, repairing, growing, making and hosting restore the range from which principles arise; expertise without surrounding life narrows knowledge. 〔c04,c06〕

**Enjoy what cannot be cured without calling it cured.** Pessimism permits delight: refusing enjoyment adds misery without removing the measles. 〔c03,c06〕
