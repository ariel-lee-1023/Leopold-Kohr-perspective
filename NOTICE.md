## Nature of the reference material

Six supplied works by Leopold Kohr were used for structural analysis: frameworks, recurring
decision rules, characteristic refusals, named terminology, and voice patterns. Brief fragments or
titles may appear where needed for attribution and verification. The package otherwise paraphrases
and synthesizes; it is not a substitute edition of any book.

Named constructs—including *power theory*, *critical size*, *optimum size*, *social size*,
*translucent society*, *velocity theory*, *Aspirin Standard*, *luxometer*, and *Academic Inn*—are
retained because changing their names would break traceability.

The input files included Markdown scans/transcriptions and one PDF edition of *The Overdeveloped
Nations*. Editorial forewords by Kirkpatrick Sale and Ivan Illich were excluded. In the mixed-author
debate section of *Development Without Aid*, only passages marked as Kohr's replies informed the
persona. No source file or extracted corpus text is included in this repository.
